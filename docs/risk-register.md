# Risk register

Status: planning. Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are Low, Medium or High, judged before mitigation. "Owner phase" is the phase and task where the mitigation is built or the risk is first tested. Evidence is cited from `docs/research/` where it exists; where a risk has no evidence yet, that is stated.

Review the register at the end of every phase. A risk is closed only when its verifying task has passed, not when the mitigation is written.

## Risks the specification lists

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Owner phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **Rendering fidelity.** Serve-time rewriting misses a URL-bearing construct (CSS `url()`, `srcset`, `@import`, inline styles, `<base>`, SVG `href`), so archived pages look broken or leak a live request. The rewriter is product-critical because we chose our own format over WARC replay ([crawling-rendering.md](research/crawling-rendering.md) section 3.4). | High | Medium | Original bytes are kept, so rewriter fixes apply to old captures. `css-tree` for CSS, never regexes. Allowlist rewrite on `parse5`. Corpus of real-world fixture pages and the XSS corpus (A-12). `default-src 'none'` and `img-src 'self'` contain misses (PoC tests C, D). Partial pages show a banner and a "Download the rest" action. `rewriter_version` recorded; optional re-process job. | 2 (A-12, A-13) |
| R-02 | **Dynamic and JavaScript-dependent sites** produce empty captures; rendered captures are not byte-identical between runs, which makes version history noisy. | High | Medium | HTTP first; extract embedded JSON (`__NEXT_DATA__`, JSON-LD) before rendering; deterministic render triggers; optional renderer with one page at a time; `on_change` compares extracted text not raw HTML; capture marks `reader_only`/`partial` honestly; per-site "always render" and "never render" flags. Authenticated apps are out of scope. | 3 (C-16, A-08) |
| R-03 | **Provider limits, cost and terms.** Quotas, rate limits and prices change; terms differ on storage ([search-providers.md](research/search-providers.md): Brave transient only, Mojeek 1 hour on Startup). | High | Medium | Per-provider `storagePolicy`; token buckets; `usage_counters` with monthly and daily caps; circuit breaker after 429 with `Retry-After`; clear UI disclosure; bring-your-own key; re-check terms before each adapter ships (open items listed in the research); SearXNG as a no-cost default. | 5 (O-01, O-07) |
| R-06 | **Index resources.** FTS5 index size and latency at 500k to 1M pages are unmeasured; common-term queries cost more as match sets grow ([search-engines.md](research/search-engines.md) section 1, point 4). Index rebuild time on a Pi is unknown. | Medium | High | `SearchIndex` interface keeps another engine possible; stop words, title-first pass, candidate cap, hot-query cache; shadow-file rebuild so search keeps working; benchmark at 50k and 500k on Pi 4/5 before promising scale (S-13, H-09); targets PT-01 to PT-04, PT-12, PT-13. If FTS5 misses, raise a Meilisearch-adapter task with its caveats (12x disk, no stemming, BUSL files). | 4 (S-13), 9 (H-09) |
| R-08 | **Large archives.** Databases, blob directories, backups, verify runs, eviction plans and link tables grow large (millions of `links` rows, 500k+ blob files). | Medium | High | Compact integer keys (D-9); derived text in blobs keeps `main.db` small (D-12); indexes designed per query; jobs rolled up; storage usage cache; chunked recompute jobs; verify is resumable and throttled; scale tests with a synthetic 500k archive (H-09); per-source and per-volume budgets. | 6 (T-06, T-10), 9 (H-09) |
| R-09 | **Crawl traps** (calendars, infinite query spaces, session IDs, repeating segments, redirect loops) exhaust storage or time. | High | Medium | Rule-based trap prevention with counters in the database, hard `maxPages`/`maxBytes`/`maxDuration`/depth ceilings per profile, per-path query-variant budget, duplicate-content streak stop, per-host budgets, trap fixture site in CI (C-04). Stop reasons are visible to the Human. | 3 (C-04) |
| R-10 | **SSRF.** `undici` `connect.lookup` is not called for IP literals (verified for decimal, hex and IPv4-mapped forms); DNS rebinding; redirects to private hosts; the renderer's own DNS ([crawling-rendering.md](research/crawling-rendering.md) sections 6.1, 2.5). | Medium | High | `ipaddr.js` unicast-only allowlist applied in `lookup` and in a separate literal check; manual redirects revalidated every hop; scheme, port and credential rules; operator allowlist only by explicit setting; SSRF-bait fixture with every PoC form; fuzzing in H-03; renderer on an internal-only network behind the guarded proxy (D-4); `live-check` also uses the guard. | 2 (A-02), 3 (C-16), 9 (H-03) |
| R-11 | **Unsafe archived content.** Stored XSS through archived HTML, CSS, SVG, ZIM content or search snippets; mutation XSS from a non-spec parser; clickjacking; popups; top-frame navigation. | Medium | High | Layered: (1) `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox` header on every response (primary), (2) iframe sandbox without scripts or same-origin, (3) parse5 allowlist rewrite, (4) listener without cookies/auth/API, (5) CSRF + `Sec-Fetch-Site` gate on the API, (6) sanitised snippets (`<mark>`/`<b>` only). Header assertions on every route class in CI. XSS corpus and unsafe-provider-result tests (A-12, O-10). Phase 9 (H-03): corpus run through archived-page, reader, ZIM and search serving and executed in Chromium and Firefox; isolation proof of concept in every browser project; see [security.md](security.md). | 2 (A-12, A-13), 9 (H-03) |
| R-13 | **Storage corruption** from power loss, bad media, interrupted writes, WAL on an unsuitable filesystem, or SD-card wear. | Medium | High | WAL with `synchronous=NORMAL` (cannot corrupt, may lose last transactions), `FULL` around user-data writes; content-addressed blobs written to temp then fsync then rename; database state final before file deletion; startup recovery; `quick_check` nightly; throttled hash scrub; backups via `VACUUM INTO`; named volumes on Linux, warnings for NFS/SMB/Docker Desktop bind mounts; SSD recommended; few writes by design (unchanged content writes nothing). | 1 (F-03, F-07), 6 (T-10), 9 (H-01, H-02) |
| R-14 | **Pi performance.** Extraction (estimated 5 to 10 times slower than the Mac, not measured), cold I/O on SD/USB, thermal throttling, memory pressure from Next.js plus Node plus workers. | High | Medium | Conservative `pi-lite` defaults (concurrency 2/1, one extraction worker, 256 MB worker heap); `resourceLimits`; CPU throttling by event-loop lag; bandwidth and disk thresholds; HTTP-only by default; benchmark-led choices; targets PT-05 to PT-11 measured on Pi 4 and Pi 5 (H-04); documented hardware guidance (SSD, 4 GB or more for the optional renderer). | 3 (C-10), 9 (H-04) |
| R-15 | **Licensing.** AGPL-3.0 project linking GPL-3.0 `@openzim/libzim` (compatible per the research, not legal advice); Meilisearch Enterprise files under BUSL-1.1 if ever added; Wikipedia text CC BY-SA 4.0 with share-alike and per-image licences; ZIM content licences vary; GPL obligations if a Docker image containing a GPL binary (Typesense/Manticore/kiwix-serve) were redistributed; every npm dependency must be AGPL-compatible. | Medium | High | Licence-audit script in CI (F-01); legal read before release flagged in H-03/H-10; attribution stored per page version and per ZIM and shown in a footer; per-image licence metadata for Wikipedia images; no redistribution of GPL images (pull upstream at install time); AGPL source link in the UI (network clause) (H-10). Meilisearch not bundled. | 1 (F-01), 8 (Z-03, Z-07), 9 (H-10) |
| R-18 | **Wikimedia API changes and limits.** RESTBase is slated for retirement; 2026 rate limits are new (10/min unidentified, 200/min identified bot); API Portal sunset ([zim-wikipedia.md](research/zim-wikipedia.md) section 5). | Medium | Medium | Core REST routes behind a `WikipediaClient` adapter with an Action-API fallback; 1 req/s, one in flight, identifying User-Agent from `NCB_CONTACT` (refuses to run without one), `Retry-After`, circuit breaker; ZIM-first preference reduces live load; re-test endpoints before release. | 8 (Z-07, Z-08) |

## Risks found in the research and the design

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Owner phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-04 | **Brave storage terms breached** (results stored or cached, snippets copied into page descriptions). | Medium | High | `storagePolicy: none` enforced at the runner, the result cache and the index writer; a database trigger rejects cache rows; session results in memory only; saving a result archives the page itself without provider data; a test scans every table for Brave data after a search (O-03). | 5 (O-03) |
| R-05 | **Provider reliability.** SearXNG upstream engines break, throttle or show CAPTCHAs to a home IP; JSON disabled by default (403); most public instances disable JSON. | High | Low | Local search never waits (D-3, O-05); `unresponsive_engines` surfaced as partial failure; fallback provider; per-provider health and circuit state; setup check for the JSON format; multi-day reliability test recorded as an open research item. | 5 (O-02, O-10) |
| R-07 | **FTS5 ranking quality.** After typo correction the Astronomy article was missing from the top five for "astronamy"; AND queries returned zero results in 12 of 30 filtered queries; Porter is English only ([search-engines.md](research/search-engines.md) section 3). | High | Medium | Single-best-correction strategy, distance-then-frequency ordering, exact-title boost, AND→OR fallback; quality suite built from `queries.json` and the spot-check table gates CI (S-05, S-15); language group routing; synonyms at query time. | 4 (S-04, S-05) |
| R-12 | **Many small files.** Derived text/reader blobs plus assets can reach millions of files; inode exhaustion, slow directory scans, slow backups. | Medium | Medium | Two-level hash sharding; zstd; check inode capacity at volume registration (warn); metrics; if H-09 shows trouble, add a packing format (append-only pack files with an index) behind the blob-store interface. | 1 (F-07), 9 (H-09) |
| R-16 | **Isolation failure.** Cookies cross ports, so script on the archive origin could use the app session; a route forgetting the CSP header; a mistaken `allow-same-origin` + `allow-scripts`; cookie tossing from the archive port ([crawling-rendering.md](research/crawling-rendering.md) section 5.3). | Medium | High | The sandbox is the isolation, not the port (decided and documented); header set on every response including errors; listener ignores cookies and never sets them; `Sec-Fetch-Site` + CSRF gate on the API; cookie name `ncb_session`; import-boundary lint (listener cannot import auth or API code); a test fails the build if any listener route lacks the headers; documentation for operators who can use a separate hostname. | 2 (A-13), 9 (H-03) |
| R-17 | **libzim blocks or crashes.** The API is synchronous; a segfault kills the host process; cold searches on huge ZIMs may block ([zim-wikipedia.md](research/zim-wikipedia.md) section 1). Also the native build step and glibc-only requirement. | Medium | High | Dedicated `zim` worker with time budgets and restart policy; package marked `degraded` after repeated failures; `ZimSource` interface so a kiwix-serve sidecar can replace it; Debian/glibc images only; pinned `LIBZIM_VERSION`; native crashes in a worker thread still terminate the process, so the fallback plan is to move the worker to a child process if H-01 shows crashes in practice. | 8 (Z-02), 9 (H-01) |
| R-19 | **Politeness and ethics.** Getting the user's IP blocked; ignoring robots; terms of service; paywalls and authentication; anti-bot measures. | Medium | Medium | Robots.txt on by default with RFC 9309 handling; identifying User-Agent with contact; one in flight per host as a database invariant; `Crawl-delay`, backoff and `Retry-After`; no bypassing of authentication, paywalls or anti-bot measures (blocked responses are recorded, not circumvented); single-page saves can override robots only through an explicit logged setting; clear documentation of Human responsibility. | 2 (A-04), 3 (C-01) |
| R-20 | **Dependency and platform risk.** `undici` 8 needs Node 22.19 or newer (crashed on 22.13.1); `better-sqlite3` 13 N-API; Drizzle 0.45 vs 1.0 RC churn and untested custom migrations; `robots-parser` last published 2023; libzim build needs `python3 make g++` and network at build; npm 11 install-script policy. | Medium | Medium | Pin Node 24 in images and `engines`; Drizzle spike in F-03 and a `Db` interface; own RFC 9309 layer over `robots-parser`; vendored/pre-fetched libzim tarball option; lockfile, Renovate-style review cadence, SBOM; dependency licence audit. | 1 (F-01, F-03), 8 (Z-01) |
| R-21 | **Address-bar and find-in-page sync with a scriptless sandboxed frame** (D-5, D-6): `load` events plus server-side frame state may mis-track redirects, native Back and joint session history; fragment scrolling inside a sandboxed cross-origin frame may differ by browser. | Medium | Medium | Spikes before building (A-14, A-18) in three browsers; shell-managed history and iframe re-creation; fallback to native find only and to documented limitations; a hash-pinned first-party script would need owner approval and is not planned. | 2 (A-14, A-18) |
| R-22 | **SSE through proxies.** The Next.js proxy route or a reverse proxy may buffer streams, breaking progressive search. | Medium | Medium | Streaming-passthrough test in F-09; `X-Accel-Buffering: no`; heartbeat; polling fallback endpoint; reverse-proxy guide with the buffering settings. | 1 (F-09), 5 (O-05) |
| R-23 | **Cross-browser behaviour untested.** Only Chromium 156 was used in the isolation PoC; Firefox and Safari handling of CSP `sandbox`, `Sec-Fetch-*` and frames is unverified. | Medium | High | Run the PoC matrix in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit in CI (H-03, H-11); document supported browsers; fail closed (the header applies regardless of browser features). | 9 (H-03, H-11) |
| R-24 | **PDF viewing** under a sandboxed document: Chromium's built-in viewer does not work, so inline PDF browsing breaks. | High | Low | PDFs offered as download plus reader text from extraction; document the limitation; revisit with a controlled pdf.js viewer on the app origin only if the Human asks (needs a design review for isolation). | 3 (C-14) — done: PDFs are stored with the original as a download (`GET /api/archive/versions/{id}/original`) and the extracted text as the reader view and for search |
| R-25 | **Eviction deletes protected content** (a planner bug, a stale cached priority, an interrupted run, an unexpected `automaticEviction` combination). | Low | High | P1 absolute rule; page-level `automaticEviction` veto; executor re-derives eligibility from references inside the transaction (cached columns are hints); planner is pure with truth-table and property tests; two-phase execution; invariant I-13 and loud logging; dry-run by default in UI; P2 needs an explicit global permit; fault-injection suite. | 6 (T-03 to T-05, T-12) |
| R-26 | **Index drift.** `main.db` and `index.db` are separate files and cannot share a transaction. | Medium | Low | Outbox in the same transaction (D-11); ordered idempotent apply; consistency check and expected count; rebuild is a first-class, tested operation (S-10); status endpoint shows drift. | 4 (S-02, S-10) |
| R-27 | **Queue stalls and growth.** Expired leases, stuck `host_state.in_flight`, unbounded `jobs` table, runaway retries. | Medium | Medium | Heartbeats and a reaper; `in_flight` rebuilt on start; finished rows pruned into counters; max attempts with dead-letter; admin view of dead jobs; I-7 invariant. | 1 (F-04), 9 (H-01) |
| R-28 | **Single-writer violation or unsuitable volumes.** A second process opening the database; NFS/SMB or Docker Desktop bind mounts breaking WAL locking. | Low | High | Writer lock file; one `server` replica enforced in docs and compose; startup check for filesystem type with warnings; renderer never opens the database (HTTP only); named volume default. | 1 (F-03, F-10) |
| R-29 | **Secrets.** Provider keys exposed, `NCB_SECRET_KEY` lost (credentials unreadable, tokens invalid), secrets in logs. | Medium | Medium | AES-256-GCM at rest; never returned by the API; redaction in logs; key generated into `/data/secrets` with mode 0600 or provided by env; loss means re-entering credentials (documented), not data loss; backups exclude the key by default. | 5 (O-07), 9 (H-02) |
| R-30 | **Programme size.** Nine phases with many cross-cutting concerns; the risk of a long period without a usable product. | High | Medium | Vertical slices: phase 2 ends with a usable offline page archive; MVP/v1/Post-v1 labels; each phase has an exit check tied to acceptance criteria; the plan allows v1 items to slip without failing acceptance; honest `docs/acceptance.md`. | All |
| R-31 | **Wrong connectivity state** without telemetry: offline shown while online (blocks fetches) or online shown while offline (slow failures). | Medium | Medium | Passive observation plus optional probe plus a manual switch (D-8); failures fail fast and degrade to the offline page; interstitials and archived pages never depend on the state to be safe. | 1 (F-15) |
| R-32 | **Privacy leakage.** Queries sent to providers without clear consent; history or search history retained against the Human's wishes; `Referer` leaks; live frames expose the Human's IP to the site. | Medium | High | Online search off until configured; disclosure acknowledged and recorded; indicator when a query leaves the machine; optional local histories with clear/disable; `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` on archive and live frames; no telemetry; live frames off unless the header probe allows and the setting is on; discovery search-driven settings default off. | 5 (O-07), 7 (D-05) |
| R-33 | **Backup and restore gaps.** Copying a live WAL database, forgetting blobs, restoring into a different layout. | Medium | High | `VACUUM INTO` backup tool; documented restore procedure with a restore-and-migrate test; index excluded from backups (rebuildable); volume manifests so blobs can be verified after restore. | 9 (H-02) |
| R-34 | **Accessibility regressions** in dense UI (tabs, progressive results, live regions). | Medium | Medium | WCAG 2.2 AA targets; axe in CI from Phase 1; `aria-live="polite"` summary, not per-result announcements; manual keyboard and screen-reader audit (H-05); reduced motion. | 1 (F-17), 9 (H-05) |
| R-35 | **Wrong system clock.** A Pi has no real-time clock; booting offline can give a wrong or past date, expiring sessions, tokens and schedules or double-running jobs. | High | Medium | Monotonic timers for leases and rate limits; clock-jump detection with logging; sessions and tokens tolerant of a backwards jump; schedules use a missed-run policy; documentation recommending an RTC or NTP when available. | 1 (F-18) |
| R-36 | **Multi-architecture build cost.** `next build` crashes under QEMU (seen in NoConMedia); native libzim build adds time; CI on arm64 runners may be scarce. | Medium | Low | Web build stage on the build platform; native arm64 runners or a self-hosted Pi runner for tests; cache layers; build matrix documented. | 1 (F-10, F-13) |
| R-37 | **Unmeasured performance.** The research benchmarks ran on a Mac; no Pi number exists; the 500k scale is untested. Risk of publishing or promising numbers that do not hold. | High | Medium | All targets labelled "to be measured" (PT-01 to PT-22); H-04 publishes real values with caveats; no marketing claims until measured; mitigations raised for each miss. | 9 (H-04) |
| R-38 | **Sensitive URLs captured automatically** by auto-caching of opened pages or discovery (password-reset links, tokens in query strings, pages behind logins that returned a public error). | Medium | Medium | Query-parameter policy and a sensitive-pattern denylist (`token`, `key`, `reset`, `session`, `auth`) skip such URLs; auto-cache is opt-in and records only what the Human opened; fetches are unauthenticated, so private pages are not captured; browsing-cache source is P4 and easy to clear. | 7 (D-05) |
| R-39 | **Discovery runaway.** Progressive expansion or search-driven discovery downloads far more than intended. | Medium | High | Hard bounds (depth, queue length, daily requests and bytes, per-site cap) independent of settings ceilings; default off or approval-required; kill switch; limits tested individually and by property tests (D-06, D-08); budgets visible in the UI. | 7 (D-04, D-06) |
| R-40 | **Source-of-truth confusion for ZIM content** in storage accounting and removal (ZIM files are huge, shared across collections and bookmarks). | Low | Medium | ZIM packages are accounted as volume usage with a priority, never auto-evicted in v1, removed only by explicit action after a reference listing; attribution retained. | 8 (Z-03, Z-09) |
| R-41 | **A secret shared between containers is used against the container that issued it.** The proxy secret is also the default secret of the renderer and SearXNG; a compromise of either (the renderer runs untrusted pages in Chromium) would hand over the secret that makes the API trust a client address. | Low | High | The API honours the secret only from the `backend` network (`NCB_API_PROXY_FROM`); SearXNG is not on it; the renderer is on `render` only. Matrix test over the route table and a text check of `compose.yml`. Separate secrets per container remain recommended in `.env.example`. Found by H-03 (HB-11). | 9 (H-03) |
